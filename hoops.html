Blue shoots at the right hoop and Gold at the left. Bump the ball with your body, or get close and hit shoot for an auto-aimed arc. Far shots are worth 3. Blue: A D to move, W jump, S shoot. Gold: arrow keys, Down shoots. With power-ups on, orbs float in: J super jump, G giant, F freeze rival, ★ perfect 4-point shot, S speed boost. Most points after 90 seconds wins. On a phone, slide your thumb left or right on the arrow pad to switch direction, and turn your phone sideways for a bigger court.
