3×3 needs 3 in a row. 5×5 needs 4 in a row. With power cards on, each player starts with 2 random cards, one use each: 🧹 Erase a rival mark, 🔄 Steal a rival mark, 🧱 Block a square with a wall, ✌️ Double to place two marks, 💣 Bomb to wipe a square and its neighbors. Using a card instead of placing a mark takes your turn, except Double.
