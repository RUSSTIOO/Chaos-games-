Five weird holes, fewest strokes wins. Drag backwards anywhere like a slingshot, then let go to putt. A longer pull is a harder hit. Water costs a stroke, sand slows you, the windmill bats you around and portals teleport you. Six misses and you pick up for 7. With power-ups on, roll over an orb: M mega putt, G ghost (pass through walls), ◎ magnet pulls you to the cup, F free stroke, C curse the rival’s next putt.
